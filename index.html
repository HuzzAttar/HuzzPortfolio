How I Work

Simple, Transparent, Step-by-Step

No confusion, no surprises. Here's exactly how your website goes from idea to live.

01

Understand Your Business

We start with a free consultation where I listen to your business, your goals, and what you want your website to achieve. No technical jargon — just a friendly conversation.

02

Requirement Discussion

I gather all the details — number of pages, features needed, your brand colors, preferred style, and any specific requirements. Everything gets documented clearly.

03

Planning & Structure

I plan the website structure, decide which pages to include, and map out the content flow so your visitors can easily find what they're looking for.

04

Wireframe

Before designing, I create a simple layout plan showing where each element will go. You review and approve before I move to visual design — no surprises.

05

UI Design

I design the full visual look of your website — colors, fonts, images, icons — aligned with your brand. You get to see and approve the design before development begins.

06

Development

I build your website with clean, modern code. This is where the design comes to life — all features, forms, animations, and integrations are implemented.

07

Testing

I test your website thoroughly on all devices — phones, tablets, computers. All buttons, forms, and features are verified to work perfectly before launch.

08

Launch

Your website goes live! I set up your domain, hosting, SSL security, and make sure everything is working perfectly. You'll get a full walkthrough of your new website.

09

Ongoing Support

After launch, I'm still here. Need to update content, fix an issue, or add a new feature? I provide continued support so your website stays fresh and effective.
